I presented at the **CaGIS 2026 Conference** of the Cartography and Geographic Information Society, held September 8–11 at Saint Louis University in St. Louis, Missouri.

My talk was *Cartographer in the Loop: Interactive Land Cover Change Screening Using Geospatial Foundation Model Embeddings*, with Koutian Wu, Rushi Bhatt, Minho Kim, and Yuhao Kang.
