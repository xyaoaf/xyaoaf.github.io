I attended the **2026 AAG Annual Meeting** in San Francisco, March 17–21, and gave two talks.

On March 18, in the GeoAI and Deep Learning Symposium session *GeoAI for Disaster Resilience II: Application of AI Foundation Models*, Jikun Liu and I presented *Instance-Level Disaster Impact Assessment from Remote Sensing: An Uncertainty-Aware GeoAI Framework Integrating Exposure Modeling*, with Akhil Anil Rajput and Ashritha Bugada.

On March 20, I presented my PhD research, *Uncovering the Geography of Personality: Linking the Big Five Traits to Built Environment Features across U.S. Urban Areas*, with Yuhao Kang, Samuel D. Gosling, Koichi Ito, Haofeng Tan, Ashmit Bhatnagar, and Somang Kim, in the session *GIScience at Cal: Celebrating Dr. John Radke's Academic Career and Retirement*, which brought together Dr. Radke's former students and colleagues from UC Berkeley.

It was my first time back in the Bay Area since last summer. I also joined the LAEP social at the UC Berkeley College of Environmental Design, and entered two photos from a four-day kayaking trip in the Everglades in the AAG Landscape Photography Competition.
