Our team won **second place** at the **2025 UT Austin Geoscience Hackathon**, organized by the Jackson School of Geosciences with support from the UT Open Source Program Office. We built an open-source tool that compares observations of the same place from different years and shows when the land surface changed, by how much, and at what scale.

We tried it on construction on the UT Austin campus, wildfire effects in California, urban expansion in Austin, and agricultural rotation in Central Texas. The project was developed by Xihan Yao and Rushi Bhatt, with mentorship from Dr. Brendon Hall.

[Project repository on GitHub](https://github.com/ktwu01/AlphaEarthHack)
