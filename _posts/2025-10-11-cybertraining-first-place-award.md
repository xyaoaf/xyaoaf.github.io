Our team won **first place** at the **2025 CyberTraining Summer School** competition, hosted by Texas A&M University. We built a framework for assessing disaster impacts that combines estimates of building damage with demographic information, so that physical damage and social vulnerability can be read together.

We presented the project on September 24, 2025, to a judging panel that included Dr. Michael Goodchild, Dr. Shaowen Wang, and Dr. Zhenhua He. The team was Jikun Liu, Xihan Yao, Akhil Anil Rajput, and Ashritha Bugada.

More about the program: [Texas A&M Cyberinfrastructure and Spatial Decision Intelligence Research Group](https://gis.tamu.edu/).
