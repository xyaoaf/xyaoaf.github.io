For my final project in **Issues in Geography** (Fall 2025), I made an interactive map of the places that have shaped my studies and my life. The project, titled *"Connecting the Dots,"* marks each place with a point that opens a short story about what I studied, lived through, or worked on there.

Making it reminded me that every point on a map holds personal stories, and that being able to move between places is itself a privilege. I built the map with Python, Folium, and Leaflet.
